# PRD — lifehacker.dev

> The product requirements for lifehacker.dev — problem, users, goals and non-goals, current features, requirements and proposed success metrics.

Derived from the README, agent docs, config and code in [`bamr87/lifehacker.dev`](https://github.com/bamr87/lifehacker.dev). Anything not stated in those sources is marked **TBD**; success metrics are proposals.

## 1. Overview / problem

A satirical-but-working Jekyll site ("Surviving life, one byte at a time") served by GitHub Pages at the apex domain lifehacker.dev, rendered by the `bamr87/zer0-mistakes` remote theme. It doubles as a headless CMS driven by a Claude Code autopilot that drafts, reviews and ships content, with a human (CODEOWNERS) as the only merge gate.

## 1.1 Missions

lifehacker.dev's direction is to become the ultimate open home for all things practical technology — data, tools, resources and transparency, gathered in one place that anyone can inspect and reuse.

1. **Provide data, tools, resources and transparency for all stakeholders, technology practitioners and the general public.** Readers, writers, operators and anyone trying to survive modern tech are first-class audiences. The aim is a shared, inspectable commons: the same hacks, the same tools, and the same trail from draft to published page for everyone.
2. **Publish hacks, tools, field notes and wire posts on a steady cadence, with satire in the framing and never in the facts.** The site stays funny without misleading: comedy is welcome in the voice, and accuracy is required in the content, especially on the wire.
3. **Run as an open, human-gated headless CMS: an autopilot drafts, reviews and ships content, CI enforces quality, and a human remains the only merge gate.** Brand, queues, ledgers and site health live in the repository as data; no change reaches the apex domain lifehacker.dev without a human merge.

These missions set the course; they do not claim the work is finished. They serve the PRD goals: a steady publishing cadence, facts that stay accurate, and quality gates with a human-only merge to main.

**Requirement**

- MR1: Constitution §1 states one direction statement and exactly three numbered missions, and the README and AGENTS.md taglines match that direction and link to constitution §1.

Feature [LHD-008](features/features.yml) traces to requirement MR1.

## 2. Target users

- Readers looking for practical tech hacks, tool reviews and news ("Wire") with a comedic voice
- The maintainer reviewing autopilot-authored PRs
- Fleet operators reusing the autopilot/CI patterns

## 3. Goals and non-goals

**Goals**

- Publish hacks, tools, field notes and wire posts on a steady cadence via the autopilot
- Keep facts accurate: satire in the framing, never the facts (wire)
- Enforce content quality with CI lints (front matter, brand, drift, links) and a Pages safe-mode build

**Non-goals**

- Patching theme bugs locally (they go upstream to zer0-mistakes)
- Auto-merging to `main` — merges are human-only
- Pinning `remote_theme` to a tag/ref

## 4. Core features (current)

- News sections Hacks / Tools / Field notes / Wire under `pages/_posts/`, with pinned `/hacks|tools|wire/:slug/` permalinks
- Machine-readable brand in `_data/brand/` (identity, voice, glossary) read by the autopilot
- Content backlog `_data/backlog.yml` with a custom merge driver
- Wire assignment editor `_data/wire/sources.yml` (sources, trust tiers, filters)
- Offline-generated cover art (`scripts/preview/`)
- CI harness: Pages safe-mode build plus front-matter, brand, drift and link lints
- Author personas in `_data/authors.yml`
- Sub-agencies held as self-contained site trees under `agencies/`, registered in `_data/agencies.yml` and excluded from this site's build
- The Wargames agency published as a collection of this site under `/wargames/`, listed on `/agencies/`

## 5. Requirements

**Functional**

- FR1: News posts require `title description date author excerpt tags` and matching `categories:`; tools also need `verdict:`, wire needs non-empty sources
- FR2: Filename date must match front-matter `date:`; no future dates
- FR3: Authors must exist in `_data/authors.yml`
- FR4: All model calls go through the fleet runner `bamr87/bamr87/.github/actions/claude-run`
- FR5 (planned): Articles originate as reviewed drafts in the hub (`bamr87/bamr87` `pages/posts/`, constitution § 23) and are written here in the voice of an SDLC-role persona from the shared fleet voice model `voices/v1` (canonical in the hub `_data/voices/voices.yml`, console edits write back by PR, schema-validated, drift-checked); nothing publishes without review
- FR6: lifehacker.dev is the content source for its sub-agencies and zer0-CMS is the platform: every agency site is a self-contained tree under `agencies/`, listed in `_data/agencies.yml` with its source and its custody (`home` or `mirror`), and arrives or is refreshed only through `scripts/agencies/import.rb`
- FR7: An agency is published as a Jekyll collection of this site under its own path (`pages/_<name>/`, served at `/<name>/`), only through `scripts/agencies/promote.rb` and only when its custody is `home`; its pages stay out of the news sections and the feed, and are listed on `/agencies/`, in the navbar and in the sitemap

**Non-functional**

- NFR1: Builds under GitHub Pages safe mode
- NFR2: Brand `avoid_phrases` hard-fail; other brand findings advisory
- NFR3: Cover-art seed order is a stable contract (append-only)
- NFR4: Performance/accessibility budgets: TBD
- NFR5: Nothing under `agencies/` is built into the apex site: `agencies` stays in `_config.yml` `exclude:`, and the harness fails if it does not
